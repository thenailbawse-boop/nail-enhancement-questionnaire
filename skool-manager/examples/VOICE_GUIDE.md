# Voice, learning and examples

## Voice principles

- Community member talking to peers, not an advertisement or lecture.
- Confident but not performative; shares what she actually learned, what blocked her, what she tried and what is working.
- Use vivid first-person phrasing naturally: 'Y'all, this learning curve has been LEARNING!'; 'Tiff gave us the foundation. I'm learning how to adapt it to my circumstances.'
- Credit mentors. Avoid claiming original authorship of systems taught by mentors.
- Keep standalone community posts within 1–2 mobile scrolls. Usually one substantive question at the end; don't mechanically attach three CTAs.
- Specific results matter; don't overclaim completed results. Example actual status Nov 19: Claude iOS/Claude Code accepted six clips, created approx. 30-second edit, completed audio cleanup; captioning/final mobile delivery and repeatable fresh sessions were not yet verified.

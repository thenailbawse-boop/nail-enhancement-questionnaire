Content Made Easy
by Tiff The AI Princess

1. Move this whole folder into your home folder (the one with your name on it).
   Not Documents, not Desktop.
2. Double-click "Start Content Made Easy".
   If your Mac says it can't be opened: open System Settings, then Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click Open Anyway. Then double-click it again.
   The first time takes about 5 minutes. Leave the window open.
3. Set your brand once. Then drop in a video.

Using Claude Code? Drag this zip into Claude Code and say:
"Unzip this into my home folder and run install-skill.sh, then run /contentmadeeasy."
After that, type /contentmadeeasy in any Claude Code chat to open it.

It cleans your video by itself. Then tap Digital Zoom or any effect.

Got an update? Replace this folder with the new one and start it the same way.
Your brand and your copied edits are saved separately, so they stay.

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing posts until YOU post it.
Your finished videos land in a folder called "Content Made Easy Videos"
in your Google Drive (or in Movies if you don't use Google Drive).

To turn it off: close the little window that opened with it.
To remove it: drag this folder to the Trash.
